This is a list of National Football League (NFL) players by total career rushing touchdowns. This list includes all players with at least 75.

Emmitt Smith is the all-time leader with 164. He also leads in postseason touchdowns with 19. Cam Newton and Josh Allen are the only quarterbacks on this list; the rest are running backs.
