Total touchdowns are passing + rushing + receiving. Positions are each player's main position. 1999 to the present: nflverse (free, open NFL data, CC BY 4.0). Before 1999: Wikipedia player pages (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against each player's career totals; players whose careers began before 1999 are included if they appear on Wikipedia's career or season touchdown leader lists or had a notable touchdown total. When an older player's Wikipedia page gives a category (often a quarterback's rushing) only as a career total, he appears in that category only in views that cover his whole career. AAFC (1946–49) statistics are not NFL statistics and are left out, as in the NFL record book.
