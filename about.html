About

Your #1 place for Josh Allen stat tracking.

A fan site, built by a Bills fan

As a lifelong Bills fan, I created this site with the purest of intentions: to help pass on the Bills fandemonium from one generation to the next.

Growing up in Western New York, I was there for it all. From the four falls to climbing back over the walls, from an illegal forward pass to a Dalton curse-breaker at last! These memories, and the greatest fan base in sports, have brought so much joy and charity to so many across the world.

This site is for them, for us… and to honor the greatest Bills quarterback ever to don the red and blue. Records? Break ’em all, Josh. This site’s for you! Go Bills!

What’s here

The site tracks Josh Allen’s career numbers, every Buffalo Bills franchise record he can be measured against, his NFL records and firsts, and where he stands on the all-time leaderboards. Before each game, the Next game page sets up the matchup: standings, the line, playoff chances and what a win would mean.

Where the numbers come from

Stats come from free, open sources, mainly nflverse, plus Pro-Football-Reference career leader lists and Wikipedia for older players. Betting lines come from ESPN’s public scoreboard feed, and weather comes from Open-Meteo. Record cards are labeled Verified once they have been checked against an official source, or Needs a second check until then.

How often it updates

The data refreshes automatically every hour. During Bills games, his live stats update every couple of minutes and are marked unofficial until the final numbers are published.

Free, and staying that way

There are no ads and no accounts. If you find the site useful, the tip jar helps cover the cost of running it, and the suggestion form is the best way to shape what gets built next.
